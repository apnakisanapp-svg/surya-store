RELEASE 11 - Visitors & reels (poora) + cookie banner aur policy
(Isme Release 10 ki sab files bhi hain. Release 10 deploy nahi kiya ho to bhi sirf ye zip upload karna kaafi hai.)

Naya:
- Visitors & reels me naye tabs: Pages, Journeys (ek visitor ki poori kahani), Cart chhode, Search, Log (login aur bina login), Mahina (CSV ke saath)
- Cookie banner me visit counting ki line
- Cookie Policy aur Privacy Policy me "Visit counting" hissa (apne aap jud jata hai agar policy tumne badli nahi thi, warna Visitors page par "Policy me jodo" button)
- Jo Reject dabate hain unhe kabhi nahi ginte
- Search me jab kuch nahi milta to ab sahi record hota hai

UPLOAD STEPS (ek-ek karke)
1. Is zip ko ek NAYE KHALI folder me extract karo. Dikhna chahiye: folders admin-web, src, test, web aur file UPLOAD-STEPS.txt
2. Kholo: https://github.com/apnakisanapp-svg/surya-store/upload/main
3. Chaaron FOLDERS (admin-web, src, test, web) drag karo. Folder ke andar ki files alag se mat drag karna.
   Check: page par naam aise dikhne chahiye: src/shared/visitpolicy.js, web/js/track.js, admin-web/js/visitors.js
4. Commit changes.
5. AWS terminal (ubuntu@...), ek line ek baar me:
     cd ~/surya-store && git pull
     sudo systemctl restart surya
     sudo systemctl is-active surya        (active likha aana chahiye)
   (npm install ki zaroorat nahi)
6. Site aur admin dono par Ctrl+F5.
7. Check: Admin > Visitors & reels me 10 tabs dikhein; site par cookie banner me nayi line dikhe.
8. Apna phone ginti se bahar rakhne ke liye site ke link ke aakhir me ?notrack=1 lagakar ek baar kholo.
9. Policy: Admin > Policies me Cookie Policy aur Privacy Policy dekh lo, aur ek baar vakil ya CA ko dikhwa lena.
