5) AWS terminal (ubuntu@...) me:
   cd ~/surya-store && git pull
   sudo systemctl restart surya
   sudo systemctl is-active surya
6) Site aur admin par Ctrl+F5. Phone me site ek baar band karke kholo.

APNE PHONE PAR TEST (link ke aakhir me daalo):
  https://suryastore.shop/?anim=light   -> light version (slow phone wala)
  https://suryastore.shop/?anim=full    -> poora version
  https://suryastore.shop/?anim=auto    -> wapas khud tay kare

ADMIN: Settings > Store options > "Animations": Auto (recommended) / Full for everyone / Light for everyone.

KYA BADLA
- Slow phone (kam RAM/cores, data saver, 2G) khud pehchana jata hai, aur site khulte waqt 1 second ki smoothness check bhi hoti hai. Light version me: glass blur band, lagatar chalne wali animations band, opening animation chhoti (~1.4 s), card tilt band. Dikhne me wahi, bas halka.
- Fast phone par kuch nahi badla.
- Cards (shop/home) me price seedha dikhta hai. Product page par price animation poori waisi hi. Card par ungli rakho to card uthta hai aur price ka number pop hota hai.
